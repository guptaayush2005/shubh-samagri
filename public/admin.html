Store ko aasani se chalane ke tips.

02 Orders & WhatsApp: Jab customer checkout karega, toh usko WhatsApp confirmation button milega aur aap Orders tab mein us customer ko 1-tap WhatsApp kar sakte hain.
03 Payments: Online payment ke liye .env file mein Razorpay test/live keys dalein. Tab tak Cash on Delivery chalu rahega.
